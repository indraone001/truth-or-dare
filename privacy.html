Privacy Policy – Truth or Dare

Last Updated: February 27, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Truth or Dare ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App.

The App is provided for entertainment purposes and does not require user account registration.

2. Information We Collect

We may automatically collect certain information when you use the App, including:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob, to display advertisements.

These services may collect advertising identifiers, device information, and usage data to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

For more information about how Google processes data, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

The App offers optional subscription-based features.

All payments are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store your payment information.

5. User-Generated Content

The App allows users to create custom truths and dares.

This content is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

6. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, we process data based on:

  • Legitimate interests (improving app performance and stability)

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share limited anonymous data with third-party service providers (such as advertising partners) solely for app functionality and monetization.

8. Data Retention

We do not maintain user accounts. Data collected through third-party services is retained according to their respective privacy policies.

9. International Data Transfers

Third-party services used by the App may process data in countries outside your country of residence.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the App and its users.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we do not maintain personal accounts, most requests should be directed to the relevant third-party providers.
